## Two schedules

Managers work in hourly slots. Makers need larger, uninterrupted blocks.

## The hidden cost

A one-hour meeting often destroys a half-day for makers due to context switching.

### Team rule

Batch meetings, protect deep-work windows, and default to async updates. For staffing dynamics that affect those blocks, read [Mythical Man-Month Notes](/classics/mythical-man-month-notes).
